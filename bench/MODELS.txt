MODES

  pipeline  The real translate step (src/6-translate-subs.py), unchanged: chunks of 50 entries with
            context before and after, a JSON schema reply, the project prompt.
            Thinking off, context 16384, 1 slot.

  native    Each entry alone, with the prompt of the model card, through the model's own chat template.
            Context 8192, 8 parallel slots. A reply with another number of lines than the English entry
            is split by words to the English line count; the trace counts these entries.

NOTES

  hy-mt2-7b           Newest translation model of the list, with official GGUF files. The card does not
                      tell Brazilian from European Portuguese.
  tower-plus-9b       The only card that names Brazilian Portuguese as its own target.
  translategemma-12b  Its chat template has broken in llama.cpp more than once (language codes dropped,
                      template rejected). A failed run or a reply in the wrong language most likely
                      comes from that.
